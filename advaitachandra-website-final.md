## The Explorations

*   **J. Krishnamurti:** Awareness, observation, and the relationship between thought and the person who is thinking.
*   **Albert Camus:** The absurd, the search for meaning, and the question of how to live when the universe offers no simple answers.
*   **Fyodor Dostoevsky:** Freedom, morality, suffering, responsibility, and the contradictions within human nature.
*   **Ramana Maharshi:** Self-inquiry and the question “Who am I?” — approached as a practice of investigation rather than simply a doctrine to accept.
*   **Osho:** Provocative, unconventional, and sometimes contradictory. Interesting less as a final authority and more as someone who challenges comfortable assumptions. [A special person who can't be described. He's just Osho.]
